Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

In short: 4DAD collects no personal data. The game has no ads, no analytics, no account and no server of ours. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

4DAD: The 4D House ("the game") is published by Nuna Games ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the game on iOS and Android and to this website.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no account and no sign-in. It contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. We do not run a server for the game, so the game sends us nothing.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, your settings and your purchase status in files on your device. These files do not leave your device. If you delete the game, the files are deleted. To delete your progress, results and settings without deleting the game, open Settings and tap "Delete data".

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional purchase that unlocks all rooms. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes the payment. The game contacts the store only to show the price, to make the purchase and to check whether your store account owns it. We do not receive your name, email, card number or any other payment details. The privacy policies of Apple and Google apply to the payment.

Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. It does not ask for permission to track you.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from any player, and that includes children.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Its fonts load from this website, not from a third party. GitHub may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is collected, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
