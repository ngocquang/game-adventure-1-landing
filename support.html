Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

You explore Dad's house in first person. Touch a mechanism to work it by hand: slide a lid, turn a box, press a button or wind a crank. Turn the brass crank to rotate the house, then look for the passages that the rotation opened.

I am stuck on a puzzle.

Tap the Hint button. Each puzzle has three hint levels, and each level is more direct than the one before. The game has no timer and no game over.

How does the notebook work?

The notebook holds the clues that you find. Open the Deduction tab, tap one card, then tap a second card to link the two clues. A correct link gives you a conclusion.

What is free, and what does the purchase unlock?

The Workshop is free to play. One purchase unlocks all four rooms of Chapter 1: the Workshop, the Living Room, the Attic and the Hidden Room. The game has no ads.

How do I restore my purchase on another device?

Open Settings and tap "Restore", or tap "Restore purchase" on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone and iPad) or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions (Android).

Will my progress move to a new device?

No. Progress is saved only on your device, and the game has no account or cloud save in this version. Your purchase can be restored on another device.

How do I start again or delete my data?

"New Game" on the title screen replaces the saved progress and keeps your settings. To delete progress, results and settings from the device, open Settings and tap "Delete data". This cannot be undone.

The motion makes me uncomfortable.

Open Settings and turn on "Comfort mode". The game then uses less shake and less motion. You can also change the text size in Settings.

Which languages does the game support?

English and Vietnamese. Change the language in Settings or with the VI and EN buttons on the title screen.
